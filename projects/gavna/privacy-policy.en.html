KEYSENSE — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

KEYSENSE ("the app") is published by Ramiro Piedrabuena ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and its purchase handle. The short version: the app never sends your music, your projects or anything about you or your computer. The only things it sends are your license key and a random installation number, to check the key. When you buy KEYSENSE, the payment is handled by Creem, our reseller.

1. What the app sends

KEYSENSE works without an internet connection. It connects only to check a license key:

  • When you activate a key, the app sends the key and a random installation number, created by the app and not linked to your name, your computer or your Microsoft account. It gets back an activation number.
  • About once a week, if you are online, an activated app checks that its key is still valid, sending the key and that activation number.
  • When you deactivate the app on a computer, it sends the key and that activation number, so that the activation is freed.

These requests go to our license server (hosted by Cloudflare) and from there to Creem, which keeps the record of license keys. Our server does not store them. Like any web service, Cloudflare records basic technical data about each request, such as the IP address and time, and keeps it for a short time for security and troubleshooting.

The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no other telemetry.

2. Information stored on your computer

To work, the app keeps a few things only on your own computer. We cannot see or access any of it:

  • Your projects — saved as files wherever you choose, and only when you save them.
  • Preferences — such as interface language, note-name system and whether to reopen your last session, in the app's own settings folder on your computer.
  • License status — when the free trial started and, once activated, your license key and activation number, in the same folder.
  • Interface state — the order of the modules and a reference to your recently opened projects, so they can be reopened.
  • Temporary MIDI files — created when you drag a chord or a slot out of the app into a folder or a music program. They are kept in a temporary folder that the app empties every time it starts.

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting your project files and uninstalling the app.

3. Buying KEYSENSE

KEYSENSE is sold through Creem (creem.io), which acts as our reseller and merchant of record: Creem processes the payment, handles sales taxes and sends you the license key and the receipt. When you buy, you give your name, email address, billing country and payment details to Creem, under the Creem Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or bank details.

From Creem we receive your name, email address, country and the details of your order. We use them only to deliver and support your license, to answer your messages, to handle refunds and to keep the records that accounting and tax laws require. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.

4. The Microsoft Store

You can download KEYSENSE for free from the Microsoft Store. Downloads and updates are handled by Microsoft, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Through Microsoft Partner Center we may receive aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as the number of installations per country) that do not identify you.

5. Links to websites

The app's license window and "About" window contain links to the purchase page and the developer's website. They open in your web browser only if you click them; from then on, those websites' own policies apply.

6. Children

KEYSENSE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you (the purchase details described in section 3), and ask us to correct or delete it, except what we must keep by law. These rights include those under Uruguayan Law No. 18.331 on the Protection of Personal Data. Write to us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the version of the app that needs the change is released.
