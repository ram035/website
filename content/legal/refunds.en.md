# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

Gavna Music Composer comes with a **15-day free trial with every feature**, so you can make sure it works for you, on your own computer, before you pay. For that reason, **purchases are final**, except in the cases below.

## When we refund

We give you a **full refund** if you ask within **14 days** of your purchase and:

- **the app doesn't work on your computer** and we can't fix it together. Write to us first: most problems have a quick solution;
- **you were charged twice**, or bought a second license by mistake.

We also refund whenever the consumer-protection law of your country gives you the right to it. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## How to ask

1. Write to **info@gavna.digital** from the email address you used to buy, or include your order number.
2. Tell us what happened. For a technical problem, include your version of Windows and what you see on screen.
3. If your case qualifies, we process the refund through Creem, our reseller, within 3 business days. The money returns to your original payment method; how long it takes to show up depends on your bank.

When a purchase is refunded, its license key is disabled on every computer.
